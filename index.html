Services

These toggles set which services belong to your room — turning one off removes it entirely and it stays off after a restart. To pause a service just for now (free its memory but keep it in the sidebar), right-click its icon in Social → Pause; it greys out and a click resumes it. Drag the sidebar icons to reorder.

Logins & cookies

Each service keeps its own persistent session - log in once, and it stays logged in across restarts until you sign out yourself. If a site offers a "security key / passkey" prompt (Windows may ask you to insert a USB key), just cancel it and use email + password; that's the site's own login option, not something this app requires.

Developer — service links

Override the address each service opens (must start with https://). Leave a box empty to use the default. Saving reloads that service.

Phone screen & control

Mirror and control your phone from the PC, over Wi-Fi. On the phone open Nishro app → More → Screen to PC → Share my screen (allow the Start recording prompt; for touch/buttons also tap Enable phone control). Then type the address it shows and open the phone window - it's a normal resizable window you can size like any other.
